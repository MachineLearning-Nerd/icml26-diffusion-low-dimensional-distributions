## Executive result

This repository is a source-pinned, claim-by-claim audit of
Diffusion Models Are Statistically Optimal for Learning Low-Dimensional
Multi-Modal Distributions. It is not an author-maintained implementation and
does not claim a full neural diffusion-model training reproduction.

The local scoped labels are:

- C1: inconclusive.
- C2: inconclusive.
- C3: inconclusive.
- C4: inconclusive locally.
- C5: toy.

The exact published Space rejudge is the authoritative competition result:
2/10 total, with C1--C3 inconclusive and C4--C5 toy. No claim is presented
here as verified or falsified.

## What is not complete scientifically

- C1 needs a new independently reviewed route before any stronger label.
- C2 and C3 have finite dependency evidence, not universal theorem proofs.
- C4 is a single finite score experiment, not a universal regularity result.
- C5 is a direct reduced-method toy, not exact-TV theorem verification.
- No full neural diffusion-model training was run or claimed.
