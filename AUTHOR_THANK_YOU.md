# Thank you

Thank you to Jingda Wu and Changxiao Cai for developing and sharing
Diffusion Models Are Statistically Optimal for Learning Low-Dimensional
Multi-Modal Distributions.

The paper gives a focused theoretical treatment of score-based diffusion
sampling for low-dimensional multimodal targets. This independent repository
tries to make its assumptions, proof dependencies, numerical controls, and
reproduction limits easier for other researchers to inspect.

This note is a courtesy of attribution and appreciation. The repository is
not author-maintained, and its scoped verdicts do not imply author
endorsement or agreement.
